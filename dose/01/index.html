METACOGNITIVE
σₕ : (x, y) → (x, −y)

Horizontal reflection. The object is unchanged; the observer’s frame inverts. This is σₕ in the Klein four-group of epistemic stances — Metacognitive, not Critical (which flips both axes) and not Non-Normative (which flips stance only).

Production rule
∀n. M(Iₙ) ⊢ Iₙ₊₁

For every state n, applying M to I at n yields I at n+1. The universal quantifier is the English phrase again and again written as a law, not a habit. A habit breaks on interruption; a law does not.

M is thinking applied to thinking. Recursion, not a one-time conclusion. Descartes proved existence once; this re-establishes it at every n.

How the four states already move

Stories first. Algebra is above. Analogy is labeled.

The semaphore is not a filing system. It is a notation for how rigorous thought already moves. It encodes a position, not a proof — and where it borrows from a discipline, it does so as analogy, not as validation.

METACOGNITIVE · σₕ · this dose
The Observer’s Mirror

A child loses a Lego and cannot find it. They have looked everywhere — under the couch, along the baseboards, through the carpet. Then they bend down, grab their ankles, and look between their legs. The room is the same room. Left is still left — but up and down have inverted. That is σₕ: (x, y) → (x, −y). The adults would look at that child as if the kid were crazy. The child was the only person in the room who performed both a physical and a cognitive act to solve the problem. Robotics uses the same principle: sensors invert perspective to map what a forward-facing orientation cannot resolve. The owl is inverted because the frame flipped: not a finding about the subject, but a finding about the instrument.

NORMATIVE · I
The Proven Ground

Stand upright, feet planted, facing forward. (x, y) → (x, y) — no transform. Newton’s laws of motion held for 200 years before Einstein showed where they break down. During those 200 years they were Normative: tested, verified, operational. The owl stands upright when the work has been done and the foundation holds. Not because it is permanently true, but because right now, the evidence supports it.

NON-NORMATIVE · σᵝ
Da Vinci’s Wings

Stand in front of a mirror. You are still upright, but left and right have swapped. That is a vertical-axis reflection: (x, y) → (−x, y). When Leonardo spent years studying birds and building machines that could not fly, he was a Non-Normative owl. Facing the other direction. Seeing what everyone else had their backs to. He failed, and left a guideline. The Wright Brothers stood on his shoulders 400 years later. Non-normative work is rigorous exploration that hasn’t finished yet.

CRITICAL · C₂
The Manhattan Moment

Do a handstand. Both axes flip. (x, y) → (−x, −y). You cannot hold it for long. The physicists of the Manhattan Project knew this owl. Their what-if calculations stopped being academic and became existential. “What if the atmosphere ignites?” Your proof turns on you — not because the analysis was wrong, because it was right, and the answer is terrifying. Psychology reaches for a felt descriptor; philosophy reaches for aporia; engineers call it a show-stopper. The owl is inverted because your own proof reversed your position.

Gödel is analogy, not proof. Incompleteness is invoked only as a picture of needing a frame outside the original one — never as a theorem about cognition. Calibration audits, methodology reviews, the moment a scientist asks whether the telescope itself is introducing the anomaly: those are σₕ in practice.
